How Search Works

AI Semantic Search

Uses advanced natural language processing to understand the meaning behind your queries. Search with natural language like "machine learning papers" or "engineering management techniques".

Keyword Matching

Traditional search that matches exact words and phrases in titles, content, categories, and tags. Great for finding specific topics or technical terms.

Advanced Filters

Narrow down results by categories, tags, or date ranges. Combine filters with both semantic and keyword search for precise results.

Relevance Scoring

Results are ranked by relevance using similarity scores. Higher percentages indicate better matches to your query. Perfect for finding the most relevant content.

Search Tips

Use natural language: "artificial intelligence research"
Try conceptual queries: "data science best practices"
Search for topics: "machine learning algorithms"
Use specific terms: "neural networks transformers"
Filter by categories and tags for focused results
