Manas Talukdar is a **builder, founder, and investor** in **AI**, **Agents**, and **Data**, with a two-decade-long career in startups in the San Francisco Bay Area. The products he has built are used worldwide across enterprises, the public sector, and critical infrastructure.

Manas is currently co-founder and CTO of Serious AI, an industrial AI startup. Previously he was Vice President of Engineering at Pryon, an enterprise AI startup, where he architected and led the development of an AI memory platform. He also drove the company's AI-native transformation and architected an agentic AI platform envisioned as the company brain. Before that, as Director of Engineering at Labelbox, he ran platform and product, and founded the machine learning engineering and applied research organizations across the US and EU. He was instrumental in the company's strategic push to support leading AI labs in developing frontier LLMs.

Prior to Labelbox, as Director of Platform Engineering at C3 AI, he founded and led an organization that built capabilities at the intersection of artificial intelligence and large-scale systems. That work produced multiple patents in enterprise AI platform technologies, including intelligent data processing and a metadata-driven machine learning feature store.

Earlier in his career, Manas spent over a decade at OSIsoft (acquired by Aveva/Schneider Electric), where he made key contributions to the world's preeminent industrial data historian, ubiquitous throughout the process industry. He also delivered several widely adopted 0->1 products in the industrial AI space.

Manas holds multiple [patents](/about/professional/patents/) in data processing and machine learning for AI. He mentors early-stage startups, speaks at industry events, reviews technical publications, and has been a Fellow of several esteemed professional organizations. He has delivered university commencement addresses and is an invited member of prestigious AI organizations.
